# Payment Methods — foodpanda Pakistan

## Accepted payment options
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)** — pay the rider in cash upon delivery.
- **Credit / Debit Card** — online card payment at checkout.
- **pandapay** — foodpanda's in-app digital wallet, used both for making payments and for receiving refunds.
- **Vouchers / promotional codes** — discount codes applied at checkout, subject to expiry and minimum order value conditions.

## Payment processing and security
- Online payments are processed through third-party payment service providers; foodpanda does not itself store customers' full card/payment details.
- Customers must ensure sufficient funds are available on the card/method used, since foodpanda takes responsibility for handling payments made on the platform — including refunds, chargebacks, and dispute resolution — provided this is reasonable and in line with the Terms.
- foodpanda reserves the right to add or remove payment methods on the platform at its discretion.

## pandapay wallet
- Refunds for card-based online payments are, by default, credited to the customer's pandapay wallet rather than back to the original card.
- pandapay allows customers to view their balance, top up, and view transaction history.

*Source: foodpanda public Terms & Conditions (Section 7 – Prices and Payments) and pandapay support pages.*
