# Company Overview — foodpanda Pakistan

**Company name:** foodpanda (Pakistan)
**Operating entity:** Delivery Hero Pakistan (Private) Limited
**Parent company:** Delivery Hero SE (Berlin, Germany)
**Industry:** Online food delivery, grocery delivery (Q-commerce), logistics
**Global brand founded:** 2012, originally launched from Singapore
**Pakistan entry:** foodpanda expanded into Pakistan in 2014 after acquiring the local player Eat Oye

## Services offered
- **Food delivery** — ordering from restaurant partners across major Pakistani cities
- **pandamart** — foodpanda's own online grocery/convenience store, delivering everyday essentials such as groceries, snacks, personal care, and household items in a short delivery window
- **Pick-Up** — customers can place an order online and collect it themselves from the vendor, in which case no delivery fee is charged
- **pandapay** — an in-app digital wallet used for payments and refunds
- **Home Chef** — a program connecting customers with home-based food businesses

## Cities served
foodpanda operates in major Pakistani cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Hyderabad, Peshawar, and others, with new cities added over time.

## Currency and pricing
All prices on the platform are shown in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and may include applicable tax (GST), depending on whether the vendor is GST-registered.
