# Delivery Policy — foodpanda Pakistan

## Delivery fees
Delivery fees are charged on every order **unless**:
- the customer selects **Pick-Up** and collects the order themselves from the vendor, or
- the customer applies a valid promotional/discount voucher that waives the fee, or
- the order qualifies for a free-delivery promotion.

The exact delivery fee depends on operational factors like the customer's location and the specific vendor, and is always shown before checkout.

## Delivery timing
- foodpanda aims to deliver as quickly as possible; pandamart in particular is built around fast delivery windows (commonly promoted as around 30 minutes) in supported cities.
- Delivery availability can depend on the time of day — some restaurants are not available late at night, though many operate 24 hours, and pandamart typically runs around the clock.

## Order tracking
Once an order is placed, customers receive confirmation (email/SMS/in-app), and can track the rider's progress in real time from the moment the order is picked up until it's delivered.

## Pick-Up option
Customers who select Pick-Up order online but collect the food directly from the vendor's location; no delivery fee applies and no fee appears on the receipt for this option. The vendor holds the order for a reasonable 20-minute window past the collection time — after that, the vendor isn't obliged to still provide it, and the customer bears the risk of any quality loss caused by their own delay.

## No-show / failed delivery
If a rider cannot reach the customer (unreachable by phone, no one available, unsafe/inaccessible location), or if the customer/recipient doesn't receive the order within 10 minutes of the rider's arrival, foodpanda can cancel the order **without a refund**.

## Delivery PIN verification
For some orders, a 4-digit Delivery PIN appears on the tracking page after the rider picks up the order; the rider may ask for this code on arrival to confirm the right person is receiving it. If PIN entry fails repeatedly, the rider may instead verify by photographing the order alongside the delivery address/unit.

## Vendor Delivery
For orders where the *vendor itself* delivers (rather than a foodpanda rider), foodpanda isn't responsible for that delivery — the customer may be asked to contact the vendor directly for delivery issues in that case.

*Source: foodpanda Pakistan public Terms & Conditions (Sections 6.8, 8.1–8.8) and foodpanda.pk FAQ content.*
