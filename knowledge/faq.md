# Frequently Asked Questions — foodpanda Pakistan

**Q: Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?**
A: Once an order has been sent to the restaurant, the customer cannot directly edit the order themselves. If a change is needed, the customer must contact the restaurant directly (before it leaves the vendor) or reach foodpanda customer support, since order edits are not possible after the food has left the vendor.

**Q: Who do I contact for a refund — the restaurant or foodpanda?**
A: For any refund related to a payment made through the app, the customer must always contact foodpanda support directly, not the restaurant.

**Q: Does foodpanda deliver 24 hours a day?**
A: foodpanda operates 24 hours in many areas, though individual restaurants set their own hours and may not be available late at night. pandamart (grocery) is generally available around the clock in supported cities.

**Q: Can I pay with cash on delivery (COD)?**
A: Yes, cash on delivery is one of the accepted payment options in Pakistan, alongside online card payments.

**Q: Can I place an order for someone else?**
A: Yes. At checkout, the customer can update the recipient's name and delivery address so the order is delivered to a different person/location.

**Q: How is the delivery fee calculated?**
A: The delivery fee depends on operational factors such as distance/location and the specific restaurant or store being ordered from. The exact fee is shown before the order is placed, and customers can filter for restaurants offering free delivery.

**Q: Do restaurants have a minimum order value?**
A: Many restaurants and stores set their own minimum order value, which is displayed during the ordering process.

**Q: How do I delete my foodpanda account?**
A: This can be requested through the in-app Help Center under Account settings, by contacting customer service to process the deletion request.

**Q: What happens if my order arrives late?**
A: The estimated delivery time shown in the app is only an estimate, not a guarantee — delays can happen due to traffic, weather, or peak-hour demand. If a delivery is significantly delayed, the customer should contact support through the Help Center; compensation (e.g. a voucher) may be offered at foodpanda's discretion for Saver/Priority Delivery delays specifically.

**Q: My order never arrived — what do I do?**
A: Contact customer support immediately through the in-app Help Center with your order ID. Support will investigate and, if the order genuinely wasn't delivered, process a refund or resolution per the refund policy.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
A: Once an order is placed and confirmed, address changes generally aren't possible for that order — the customer would need to contact support quickly to see if a change is still feasible before the rider is dispatched, or cancel and reorder if the vendor hasn't accepted yet.

**Q: Why was my order automatically cancelled?**
A: This can happen if the rider couldn't reach the customer/recipient (phone unreachable, no one available) or if the customer doesn't collect the order within about 10 minutes of the rider's arrival — in these "no-show" cases, no refund is issued.

**Q: Is there a helpline number I can call?**
A: foodpanda's primary support channel is the in-app Help Center (Profile → Help Center, or My Orders → Get Help for live orders) rather than a publicized general phone helpline; live-order issues get faster in-app chat support.

**Q: Can I order alcohol or tobacco through foodpanda?**
A: Tobacco products may be available only where legally permitted, and only to customers who can prove they meet the statutory legal age; riders can request ID and refuse delivery otherwise. Alcohol is treated as a restricted item and isn't offered where prohibited by law.

**Q: I got someone else's order / wrong items — what now?**
A: Contact customer support right away through the Help Center; photographic proof may be requested. If confirmed, foodpanda compensates for the order or the affected items per the refund policy.

**Q: Does foodpanda charge a "platform fee" or hidden charges?**
A: Any additional charges — delivery fee, small-order fee, service/platform fee — are itemized and shown in the price breakdown before checkout; the customer agrees to the final total shown before placing the order.

**Q: Can restaurants refuse to fulfill my order?**
A: Yes — a vendor can decline to accept an order (e.g. if it's outside their capacity or delivery zone). If a vendor hasn't accepted the order yet, the customer can cancel it for a full refund.

**Q: The restaurant I want is showing as closed — why?**
A: Each vendor sets its own operating hours, and the app only shows restaurants currently open (or accepting orders) for a given location and time. If a restaurant appears closed, it's outside its own business hours or temporarily paused, not a foodpanda-wide issue.

**Q: My discount voucher isn't working — why?**
A: Vouchers are subject to conditions — validity period, minimum order value, specific restaurants/cities, and they generally can't be combined with other vouchers or promotions. If a voucher fails, the checkout screen usually shows the specific reason (expired, minimum not met, etc.).

**Q: Can I schedule an order for later instead of ASAP?**
A: Yes — foodpanda offers Scheduled Delivery, letting a customer pick a future time slot for the order to be prepared and delivered instead of ordering for immediate ("ASAP") delivery.

**Q: Can I tip my rider?**
A: Yes — at checkout, customers can optionally add a Rider Tip, which goes fully to the rider once the order is delivered. This isn't available for cash-on-delivery orders.

**Q: Is it safe to save my card details in the app?**
A: Saved payment details are stored securely by foodpanda's third-party payment processor in encrypted form; foodpanda itself does not store full card numbers.

**Q: Can I order for a group and split the bill?**
A: foodpanda supports group-ordering functionality in some markets, letting multiple people add items to one shared cart; whether this feature and any bill-splitting option is available depends on the app version and city.

**Q: What's the minimum age to use foodpanda?**
A: Users must be at least 18 years old to register and use the platform; users under 18 need a parent or legal guardian's consent, and that guardian takes responsibility for charges made.

**Q: Can I order without using the app, just from the website?**
A: Yes — foodpanda's services are available both through the mobile app and the foodpanda.pk website; both use the same account and ordering system.

**Q: The app is slow or keeps crashing — what should I do?**
A: This is usually a device/network or app-cache issue rather than an account issue — try updating the app to the latest version, restarting it, or checking your internet connection; if the problem persists, it can be reported through the in-app Help Center.
