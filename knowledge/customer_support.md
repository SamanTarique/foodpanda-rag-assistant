# Customer Support — foodpanda Pakistan

## Primary support channel
foodpanda directs most order-related issues through the **in-app Help Center**:
- Go to Profile → Help Center for general queries.
- For an active/live order, go to My Orders → Get Help, where live chat-style support is available.

## Privacy / data protection contact
For data protection or privacy-related requests specifically, customers can email: **dpcpakistan@foodpanda.pk**

## Vendor and rider support
- Restaurant/shop partners have their own vendor support channel (vendor portal) separate from the customer-facing Help Center.
- Riders use a separate rider-support system through the rider app for delivery-related issues.

## Refunds and payment disputes
Customers are told to route any payment-related refund query through foodpanda support (not the vendor), since foodpanda is responsible for managing refunds on the platform.

## Social media
foodpanda maintains official social media pages (Facebook, Instagram) mainly for announcements and promotions; for order support, customers are redirected back to the in-app Help Center rather than social media DMs.
