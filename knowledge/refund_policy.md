# Refund & Cancellation Policy — foodpanda Pakistan

## General rule
A customer can cancel an order and is entitled to a full refund **only if the vendor has not yet accepted the order**. Once a vendor accepts the order, the customer forfeits the delivery if they still choose to cancel — no partial or full refund is issued in that case, whether payment was online or cash-on-delivery.

## Cash-on-Delivery (COD) specific rule
If a customer repeatedly cancels COD orders after vendor acceptance, foodpanda may remove the COD option from that customer's available payment methods going forward. COD is also not available for the pandago parcel-delivery service.

## How approved refunds are issued
Where a customer is legitimately entitled to a refund:
- For orders paid by **credit card, debit card, or COD**, the refund is issued **by default to the customer's pandapay wallet**.
- If the order was paid using another online method (e.g. PayPal, Google Pay) or the customer has no pandapay account, the refund is credited back to that same original payment method instead.
- foodpanda may ask for extra supporting information or documents before releasing a refund — for example, photographic proof of a physical receipt for a Pick-Up order — and can decline to process the refund if this isn't provided.
- The exact refunded amount is determined at foodpanda's discretion.
- If a refund still hasn't arrived, the customer should first check their bank/card statement, since bank processing can add delay, before contacting customer support.

## Transferring a pandapay refund to your original payment source
A refund credited to pandapay can optionally be transferred back to the original card used, except for COD orders — for COD refunds, the customer must instead designate a bank account, and that bank transfer can take roughly 7–10 business days. foodpanda can refuse a transfer-out request where needed for risk-management or regulatory reasons.

## Wrong / missing / defective items
If an order arrives incomplete, incorrect, or damaged, the customer should contact support immediately (photo proof may be requested); if foodpanda determines the order wasn't delivered in satisfactory condition, compensation is issued for the order or the affected portion of it.

## Fraud and account safety
foodpanda reserves the right to cancel an order, or to suspend/deactivate/terminate an account, if it reasonably suspects fraudulent behavior connected to that account or order.

## pandago (parcel delivery) refund cap
For items lost or damaged during a pandago delivery, foodpanda's refund is capped — generally up to PKR 5,000 per item (case-by-case), and up to PKR 10,000 for customer-to-customer pandago claims overall.

## Rider Tips
If an order is cancelled *before* delivery, any Rider Tip already paid is refunded back to the original payment method (this can take 5–7 business days, longer for overseas banks). Once an order has been delivered, the Rider Tip is **not refundable**.
