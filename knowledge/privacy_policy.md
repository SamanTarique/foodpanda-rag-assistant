# Privacy Policy (Summary) — foodpanda Pakistan

## Who is responsible for your data?
foodpanda Pakistan and its parent company, Delivery Hero SE (based in Berlin), jointly determine how customer personal data is processed. Both entities are considered jointly responsible for shared data-processing activities, and customers can raise data-related requests with either.

## What counts as personal data?
Personal data is any information that can identify a customer directly (e.g., name) or indirectly (e.g., through an account/user ID linked to profile details).

## Customer rights
Customers can request to access the personal data foodpanda holds about them, and can also request account deletion through their profile settings on the platform.

## How to contact the Data Protection team
Any privacy-related question or data request can be sent to foodpanda Pakistan's data protection contact: **dpcpakistan@foodpanda.pk**. foodpanda may charge a reasonable fee for handling certain data access requests, and will inform the customer of any such fee in advance.

## Data security
foodpanda states it uses reasonable measures to protect personal data from misuse, unauthorized access, or loss, and will delete personal data it no longer needs to retain under the policy — noting that no transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

## Cookies
The foodpanda website uses cookies; continuing to use the site is treated as consent to their use, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## Categories of personal data collected
foodpanda groups the data it processes into: profile/master data (name, email, phone, password, delivery addresses, age), delivery data (order details, addresses, recipient info for pandago), order history, location data (address and device GPS coordinates), device/access data (device ID, OS, IP address), customer care data (support conversation records, call recordings), marketing data, and payment data (payment method, encrypted/pseudonymized card info — full card numbers are not stored by foodpanda itself).

## Account login options
Customers can register/login using email + password, or via single sign-on with Facebook, Google, or Apple. In each case foodpanda only receives basic profile info (name, user ID, email) with the customer's explicit consent, and never receives the password for those third-party accounts.

## Fraud detection
foodpanda runs automated monitoring to detect suspicious account activity; if an automated decision negatively affects a customer's account, they can request a human review by emailing the data protection contact.

## Marketing communications
Customers only receive marketing emails/SMS/push notifications if they opted in at signup, and can unsubscribe at any time via the email footer link or by writing to the data protection contact.

## Data retention
Data is generally deleted once its purpose is fulfilled, subject to legal minimum retention periods (e.g. tax records must be kept several years). Accounts inactive for more than 3 years are deleted, with prior email notice sent to the customer.

## Who data may be shared with
foodpanda states it does not sell personal data to third parties. Data may be shared with: other Delivery Hero group companies (under intra-group data transfer agreements), audited service providers/data processors (e.g. cloud hosting, IT), professional advisors (lawyers, tax consultants) under contract, and law enforcement/regulators where legally required.

*Source: foodpanda Pakistan public Privacy Policy (foodpanda.pk/contents/privacy.htm), last updated December 2022.*
