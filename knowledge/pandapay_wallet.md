# pandapay Wallet — Terms Summary

## What pandapay is
pandapay is foodpanda's own closed-system digital wallet, available to any customer with a valid foodpanda account and Pakistani residency (18+ years old).

## How money gets into the wallet
Funds can only enter a pandapay wallet in two ways:
1. **Refunds** from cancelled/returned online orders.
2. **Online top-ups** via debit or credit card.
Cash cannot be deposited directly, and the wallet cannot be topped up in a way that pushes the balance over the cap.

## Balance limit
The wallet balance can never exceed **PKR 10,000** at any point — top-ups or refunds that would push it over this cap are not allowed.

## Using the balance
- Wallet funds can only be spent on vendors listed on the foodpanda platform.
- If an order costs more than the wallet balance, the balance is used in full and the remainder is charged to the chosen payment method (card or COD).
- If an order costs less than the balance, only the order value is deducted; the rest stays available for future orders.

## Withdrawing / transferring funds
- The balance **cannot be withdrawn as cash**.
- Refunded amounts can optionally be transferred back to the original payment source (except COD refunds, which instead go to a designated bank account, taking about 7–10 business days).
- Amounts added purely through online top-ups (not refunds) **cannot** be transferred out.

## General rules
- No interest is paid on the wallet balance.
- The wallet is non-transferable and each user may hold only one pandapay account.
- foodpanda charges no fee to open or use a pandapay wallet (subject to change with notice).
- The wallet doesn't expire as long as the account stays active; if the account is closed voluntarily, remaining balance should be spent before closing. If foodpanda terminates the account for cause (e.g. fraud, policy violation), any remaining balance may be forfeited.

## COD "change" feature
When paying cash-on-delivery, a customer can choose to receive their exact change instantly into their pandapay wallet instead of handling loose cash from the rider.

## Unauthorized transactions
If a pandapay account is used without the owner's permission (e.g. stolen login), the account holder is responsible for any transactions made before they report the issue to foodpanda. Affected users are advised to report to both the police and foodpanda support.

*Source: foodpanda Pandapay User Account Terms and Conditions (foodpanda.pk/contents/pandapay-user-account-terms-and-conditions), published 22 March 2023.*
