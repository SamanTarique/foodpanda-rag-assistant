# pandamart — foodpanda's Grocery Delivery Service

## What is pandamart?
pandamart is foodpanda's own online grocery and convenience-store service, run through dark stores (dedicated fulfillment warehouses) rather than partner restaurants. It lets customers order everyday items directly through the foodpanda app.

## Product categories
pandamart typically stocks groceries, beverages, snacks and confectionery, dairy, fresh produce, personal care, household essentials/cleaning supplies, baby products, and over-the-counter pharmacy-type items.

## Key features
- **Speed:** pandamart is positioned around fast delivery, often promoted as around 30 minutes in supported areas.
- **Availability:** Many pandamart dark stores operate 24/7.
- **Ordering flow:** Customers open the "Shops" tab in the app, browse a store's inventory, add items to the basket, and check out the same way as a restaurant order.
- **Minimum order:** Some pandamart locations have a minimum order value or no minimum at all, shown on the store's page.
- **Payment:** pandamart orders can be paid via card, wallet (pandapay), or cash on delivery depending on availability in that city.
